# Drift

An interactive particle field. Move across the page and the particles spiral toward your pointer, leaving trails that shift color.

Open [index.html](./index.html) in a browser. There is no install or build step.

- **Particles** and **Force** change the density and how hard the field curls in
- **Color** shifts through Ember, Tide, Ash, or Dusk
- **Background** switches Void, Graphite, Depth, and Paper
- **Reset** clears the trails and reseeds the field (or press R)
- **Save image** downloads a PNG of the field
- Click or tap for a burst that curls back in

Your settings stay in this browser.
